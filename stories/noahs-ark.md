## The Builder
An old man with a long grey beard and simple wool robes stands on a wooden scaffold, hammering a beam into the vast half-built hull of an enormous wooden ark, while neighbours watch from the dusty plain below.
Motion: Slow upward crane shot from the curious onlookers to the old man hammering; wood dust drifts in the sunlight.

## Two by Two
A long line of animals in pairs (elephants, lions, giraffes, sheep, and doves) walks up a wide wooden ramp into the ark under a darkening sky.
Motion: Steady tracking shot alongside the procession; the animals walk calmly as wind picks up.

## The Flood
The great ark floats alone on a vast, storm-tossed ocean beneath towering black clouds and sheets of rain, a single warm lantern glowing in its window.
Motion: Slow push-in on the ark as it rises and falls on heavy waves; lightning flickers in the distance.

## The Dove Returns
Inside the dim ark, the old man holds out his hands as a white dove flies through an open window carrying a fresh olive leaf in its beak.
Motion: The dove glides gently into frame and lands on his hands; soft light pours through the window.

## The Rainbow
The ark rests on a green mountaintop after the rain; the family and animals step out onto wet grass as a brilliant rainbow arches across a clearing sky.
Motion: Slow wide pull-back revealing the whole mountaintop and the rainbow; clouds part and sunlight spreads.
